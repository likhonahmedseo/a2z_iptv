True Single-Tenant Architecture

What a Dedicated Server in India Actually Means

The Indian hosting market mislabels products. Some providers call VPS instances “dedicated.” Others sell cloud compute under bare-metal names. A real dedicated server in India does three things:

Allocates all hardware to one client

No hypervisor shares the CPU, RAM, or storage with another account. Every physical cycle belongs strictly to your operating system.

Delivers predictable throughput

Performance stays stable because no neighboring tenant competes for resources. I/O wait times remain flat even under sustained peak loads.

Provides full hardware control

You get root access, KVM (IPMI) out-of-band management, and the ability to install any operating system or custom ISO directly.

A dedicated server is the right choice when performance variance is not acceptable — fintech transaction engines, gaming backends, high-volume SaaS APIs, and media delivery platforms all fall into that category.

Why Bare Metal Outperforms VPS for High-Load Workloads

Virtualization adds a software layer between your application and the hardware. That layer introduces latency measured in microseconds for each I/O operation. At low traffic volumes, that cost is invisible. At high concurrency — 10,000 simultaneous database reads, 50,000 API requests per minute, or 5Gbps sustained network output — that cost compounds.

A bare metal server in India removes the hypervisor entirely. Your application writes directly to the NVMe controller, reads directly from the CPU cache hierarchy, and sends packets directly through the NIC. For workloads where throughput consistency matters, there is no substitute.

Regulatory Framework

Compliance and Regulatory Requirements for India-Hosted Infrastructure

Navigate Indian data sovereignty frameworks seamlessly with in-country physical infrastructure.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act)

The DPDP Act 2023 governs how personal data of Indian citizens gets collected, stored, and processed. The Act grants data principals the right to access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal. For data fiduciaries (the businesses that process the data), compliance requires:

  • Defined purpose limitation for data collection
  • Documented data processing agreements with processors
  • Breach notification to the Data Protection Board of India within required timelines
  • Data localization for certain categories of sensitive personal data

Hosting inside India means your data storage layer already satisfies the geographic dimension of localization. You avoid the added legal complexity of cross-border data transfer agreements for data that never leaves the country.

IT Act 2000 and Intermediary Guidelines 2021

The Information Technology Act 2000 defines the legal framework for electronic commerce and digital infrastructure in India. The 2021 Intermediary Guidelines establish specific obligations for significant social media intermediaries and other digital platforms, including grievance officer requirements, content takedown timelines, and government data access requests.

In-country hosting reduces the friction around these obligations. Data requests from Indian regulatory authorities reach infrastructure that sits inside Indian jurisdiction, processed under Indian law without cross-border legal cooperation delays.

RBI, IRDAI, and SEBI Data Localization Requirements

Sectoral regulators have stricter requirements than the general DPDP framework:

  • RBI: Payment data for Indian transactions must be stored only in India. This applies to all payment aggregators, banks, and fintech platforms licensed by RBI.
  • IRDAI: Insurance data for Indian policyholders requires in-country storage.
  • SEBI: Certain trading and market data records require local storage and defined retention periods.

For businesses in these verticals, a dedicated server in India is not an option — it is a compliance requirement. Our India infrastructure satisfies all three sectoral frameworks.
