Canada restream FAQ

Canada Restream FAQs

Can I combine Canadian sources with US channels?
Include both countries in your request. We’ll confirm the available categories and whether the proposed package covers your selection.
Are French Canadian channels the same as the France category?
No. They are different content selections. Specify Canadian French-language feeds when sending your requirements.
Can I request sports, news, or entertainment feeds?
Yes. Send the exact channel list so we can check the available selection. A Canada package does not automatically include every broadcaster or event.
Can I use these sources outside Canada?
Tell us your delivery location and intended audience before ordering. We’ll review the applicable access and distribution terms for the offer.
Is a Canadian server included?
Canadian content identifies the source category, not the hosting location. Include any server-location requirement separately in your inquiry.
