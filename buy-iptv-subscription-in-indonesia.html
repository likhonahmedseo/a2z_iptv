Electronic Program Guide (EPG) Timing

Check Program Times Across Indonesia

Indonesia has three time zones. Use your local zone when checking an electronic program guide, or EPG.

The table shows the same moment in three locations:

Location Time zone Example local time
Jakarta WIB, UTC+7 8:00 p.m.
Denpasar WITA, UTC+8 9:00 p.m.
Jayapura WIT, UTC+9 10:00 p.m.

This is a time-conversion example, not an event schedule. The zones follow the BMKG national time reference.

For a late-night broadcast, check the date as well as the time. A program can start before midnight in one zone and after midnight in another.

If your guide shows the wrong time, send us your city, app name, device, and a screenshot. Include the channel and program so our support team can review the issue.
