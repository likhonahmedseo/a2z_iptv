Complete Your Japan Viewing Check

Pick three live channels and one movie or series episode. Then follow these checks:

  • Open your requested programs. Check the exact station, episode, or live feed.
  • Select your audio. Listen to the language track you plan to use.
  • Test subtitles. Check availability, readability, and timing.
  • Read the guide. Check Japanese characters and program descriptions where provided.
  • Compare dates and times. Match the listing to the program playing on screen.
  • Watch during your usual hours. Note repeated pauses, errors, or missing sound.

For late-night viewing, check the date as well as the start time. A listing for 00:30 belongs to the early hours of that calendar date. This is a reading example, not an event schedule.

Send us the channel, device, app, and time if a guide entry does not match playback. Avoid changing several settings at once, since that can make the cause harder to identify.

Our service includes HD, Full HD, and 4K options where available. Actual quality depends on the source, device, player, and connection. Ask about the trial’s screen allowance before testing two or three devices at once.
