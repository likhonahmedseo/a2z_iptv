Korean and International TV

Need Korean news, local entertainment, or television from home while living in South Korea? Ask us to check the exact networks you want.

For requests involving KBS, MBC, SBS, EBS, tvN, or JTBC, confirm each channel before payment. This list identifies possible requests, not a promise that every network is included.

Sports, Movies, and Series

Share the sports, competitions, movies, or series that matter to you. A general sports category does not confirm access to every match or event.

We recommend testing the relevant channel during your trial. For movies and series, check the title, playback, and available language options rather than choosing by catalog size alone.

TV Guide, Audio, and Subtitles

An electronic program guide, or EPG, shows program schedules. Video on demand, or VOD, lets you select available movies and episodes.

During testing, check your required audio tracks and subtitles. Also check that the guide displays schedules correctly for Korea Standard Time. Features can depend on both the supplied content and your player.
