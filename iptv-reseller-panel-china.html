Credit Calculation

See the Cost Behind Each Sale

Our listed subscription plans use this calculation:

Months of access × connection allowance = credits used.

Ten one-month accounts with one connection each use ten credits. Their allocated credit cost is €20 at the smaller-pack rate or €15 at the 500-credit rate.

You purchase a complete credit pack, then use its balance for supported account actions. Include payment fees, currency conversion, and your own business expenses when setting retail prices.
