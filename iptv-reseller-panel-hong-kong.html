Credit Calculation

Calculate an Account Before You Quote

Months × connections = credits required.

A three-month account with two connections uses six credits. Eight accounts with that setup use 48 credits, leaving two from a 50-credit pack.

Use this calculation to price the subscription you plan to sell. Your retail revenue is not the same as profit.
