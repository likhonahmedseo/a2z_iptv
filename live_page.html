Plan for Active Usage

Your customer total is only part of the capacity decision. An account that is not watching does not represent the same demand as an active stream. Your panel configuration and the way it requests sources also affect usage.

Share your expected active demand and the number of servers accessing the source. We can discuss the plan’s connection rules before you commit. Avoid assuming that one customer account always equals one restream connection.

Prepare for Busy Viewing Periods

Sports fixtures and evening viewing can change demand. Test the categories your audience uses and review activity during the hours that matter to your business.

For an existing operation, use your actual usage records to guide the discussion. For a new setup, start with the capacity you can justify and review upgrade terms before expanding.

Get quick, transparent answers about restream capacity, panel compatibility, and access methods.
