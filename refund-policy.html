Policy Summary

A Fair Refund Policy for Digital IPTV Service

Digital IPTV access is delivered quickly, so refund decisions must be based on timing, service status, evidence, and account behavior. These are the main rules customers should understand before opening a refund request.

7 Day Window

Customers are entitled to request a full refund within the first 7 days after subscribing to A2Z IPTV.

Service Issue Review

Refunds are granted when the service is not working as advertised and support can verify the issue.

Evidence Required

Screenshots, error messages, device details, and support history help the team review your case faster.

Policy Protection

Refund requests may be denied if terms of service are breached or A2Z IPTV policies are violated.

Refund Eligibility

When an A2Z IPTV Refund Can Be Approved

A refund review focuses on whether the IPTV service was delivered and whether it worked as advertised during the first 7 days. Support may first help troubleshoot app setup, playlist loading, device compatibility, internet stability, or account configuration before confirming the outcome.

Full refund requests must be opened within 7 days of the subscription purchase.

The service problem must relate to A2Z IPTV not working as advertised.

Evidence such as screenshots, error messages, or short screen recordings may be required.

Multi-month cancellations can qualify for a prorated refund based on remaining unused time.

Non-Refundable Cases

When Refunds May Be Denied

A2Z IPTV keeps refund reviews fair, but the refund policy does not cover every situation. Clear evidence and honest account use matter.

Outside the 7 day window

Full refund requests should be made within the first 7 days after subscription. Later cancellation requests may be reviewed under the prorated rule for multi-month plans.

No issue evidence

If support cannot verify the problem with screenshots, errors, setup details, or troubleshooting history, the request may be declined.

Terms or policy breach

Account misuse, policy violations, unauthorized sharing, abusive behavior, or breach of terms can remove refund eligibility.

Device or internet limits

Problems caused only by unsupported apps, incorrect setup, weak internet, VPN conflicts, or local device limitations may need setup help instead of a refund.

What to Send

Help Support Decide Faster

A strong refund request is specific. The more complete your first message is, the easier it is to confirm whether the issue is service-related, setup-related, or payment-related.

Order Details

Send your order number, payment date, package name, username, and contact email if available.

Device Details

Tell support your device, IPTV app, internet type, VPN use, and whether other apps work normally.

Problem Evidence

Attach screenshots, visible error messages, affected channel names, loading failures, or short videos when possible.

Support History

Mention what troubleshooting was already tried so the team does not repeat the same setup steps.

Multi-Month Plans

Prorated Refunds for Early Cancellation

If you purchased a multi-month subscription and want to cancel before the end of the term, A2Z IPTV may issue a prorated refund based on the remaining time left on the subscription.

Prorated refunds are reviewed after support checks the purchase date, active term, usage status, policy compliance, and cancellation request date.

Refund Payment

Original Payment Method and Timing

All approved refunds are issued through the same payment method used for the original purchase whenever possible. This keeps the payment record traceable and helps prevent payment disputes.

Please allow up to 7 business days after approval for A2Z IPTV to process the refund. Your bank, card issuer, wallet, or payment provider may take additional time before the money appears in your account.
